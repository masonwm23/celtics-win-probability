OPPONENT CONTEXT (PRE-REGISTERED)
==============================================================================

  Every opponent measure is computed AS OF THE GAME DATE from
  prior games only. A full-season record includes games played
  after the one being predicted, which would be a leak.

  Variants fixed in advance, all reported.

  Tier 5: Celtics-specific plus opponent context              0.7627   0.1985  11.9%  0.1145

  Variant E: opponent recent form only (last 10 games)        0.7760   0.1861  17.3%  0.0891
  Variant F: Celtics minus opponent differential only         0.7934   0.1817  19.3%  0.0875

  NO opponent formulation here improves on tier 3.

  DO NOT READ THAT AS A FINDING ABOUT OPPONENTS.
  Phases 6 and 7 established that these tiers are
  CONTAMINATED. Every measure above is constant
  within a game and takes a distinct value in nearly
  every game, so the tree can isolate one training
  game into a pure leaf and memorise it: tier 5's
  training Brier is 0.0076 against a 0.2290 baseline.
  A column of RANDOM numbers at the same resolution
  does the same damage, and the damage scales with
  the number of distinct values for both.

  The usable measurement is in reports/
  phase7_clean_tests.txt: at about 5 distinct values
  opponent context is neutral to slightly positive,
  and in the linear model it is a small improvement.
  Run scripts/16_run_clean_tests.py.
