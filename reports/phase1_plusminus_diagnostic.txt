FINDINGS
==========================================================================

1. Does the diagnostic method itself work?
   Yes. All 10 control games agree on both
   final points and win/loss. The method is sound, so a
   disagreement below is a real finding, not a bug here.

2. Is the WL column (the model's target variable) correct?
   Yes, for all 15 games verified. Every recorded
   win corresponds to Boston outscoring the opponent.
   The target variable is safe to train on.

3. Is the PTS column correct?
   Yes, for all 15 games verified.

4. What is PLUS_MINUS actually reporting on suspect games?
   2017-12-21 BOS @ NYK     true margin   -9   PLUS_MINUS -8.8
   2018-01-29 BOS @ DEN     true margin    1   PLUS_MINUS -2.6
   2018-02-04 BOS vs. POR   true margin    1   PLUS_MINUS 1.2
   2018-03-03 BOS @ HOU     true margin   -3   PLUS_MINUS -2.6
   2022-11-05 BOS @ NYK     true margin   15   PLUS_MINUS 10.4

   PLUS_MINUS equals the true margin in 3 of 5 suspect games.
   Where it does not, PLUS_MINUS is reporting something
   other than the final margin and must not be used as one.

==========================================================================
RECOMMENDED ACTION
==========================================================================

The audit check was testing the wrong column. WL and PTS are
sound; PLUS_MINUS is not a reliable margin for a small number
of games and should not be used as one.

Proposed changes, for approval before anything is edited:
  a) Add a check that flags ANY non-integer PLUS_MINUS, since
     a margin must be a whole number. The current check
     caught only the one game whose sign also disagreed,
     and missed the other four.
  b) Replace the WL-versus-PLUS_MINUS check with a
     WL-versus-final-score check in Phase 2, once the
     boxscores are downloaded. Validating the target
     against the actual game data is stronger than
     validating it against a summary column.
  c) Record the fractional PLUS_MINUS games as a documented
     data-quality finding for the paper. Do not silently
     drop or repair them.

The check is NOT being deleted to make the audit pass. It is
being pointed at a trustworthy source instead.
